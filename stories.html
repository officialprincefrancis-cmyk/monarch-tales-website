Every family has a story only they could tell.

A few glimpses of how the small, familiar things a family shared became part of their finished Monarch Tale.

Ronyem & Isom · A little homecoming

When the lock clicked, they knew Mum was home.

Real detail. Mum shared how Ronyem would run to the door calling for her when he heard the lock; Isom would follow.

What it held. Recognition, closeness and the excitement of being together again.

In the book. The two children hurry through the room to welcome Mum home. The words and scene are shaped into a connected story, not copied as a transcript.

Knox · A Christmas keepsake

A Christmas story with Knox at the centre.

Real detail. The cover title reads You Are My Christmas Sunshine, with a personalised storybook illustration of Knox.

What the book shows. Knox is pictured in a Christmas setting, holding an ornament beneath the tree.

In the book. A printed hardcover created around one child and a family’s Christmas story.

Ronyem & Isom · At church together

A room full of music. A child finding her own pace.

The printed scene shows the church full of music and people singing. Ronyem looks around at all the faces; Isom stays close to Mum and watches the room before joining in.

Family routines, relationships and faith can shape a story when parents choose to share them. Every commission begins by listening to what matters to that family.
