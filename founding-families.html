Clear expectations

What founding families should know.

Does applying guarantee a place?

No. We review every application against current capacity, supported delivery and whether the requested story fits the Founding Families scope.

When does production begin?

Acceptance reserves entry into the programme, but active production begins according to your confirmed batch. We do not promise immediate production.

What does character approval mean?

You approve the overall storybook identity and visual direction before scene production. Once approved, that direction becomes locked. Complete redesigns are outside the included corrections.

Are revisions unlimited?

No. The edition includes one consolidated story-review round and two consolidated illustration-correction rounds. Additional work can be quoted separately.

Can the book include siblings or twins?

Yes. The story can centre more than one child and preserve each child's individual personality within their shared family world.
