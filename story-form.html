Monarch Tales Creative Intake

Story Creation Form

Now let’s shape the heart of the story.

This stage gathers the deeper details that help us create a Monarch Tale that feels warm, meaningful, and beautifully connected to the child.

Let’s remember them properly.

This is more than a questionnaire. It is a gentle pause to notice the phrases, habits, relationships, and ordinary moments that make your family unmistakably yours.

Write as you speak. Honest, imperfect answers often give us the most beautiful material.

Most families take 25–35 minutes. A sentence or a voice-like memory is enough—you do not need to sound like a writer.

Child and family details are used only to create your story and are treated with care.

Please complete this form in one sitting. Your answers may not be saved if you leave the page.

Part 1 of 9 Take your time
Part 1 · The beginning

Your family and this keepsake

First, help us connect these memories to the right family and understand why this book matters now.

Part 2 · Meet the child

Who are we remembering?

Add every child who is central to this story. Twins and siblings each receive their own space—because being in the same family never means being the same person.

Picture them walking into the room. What would we notice first—their words, their movement, their eyes, their laugh, or the way everyone responds to them?

Child 1

If the book centres on one child, simply continue. Supporting siblings can still be included in the family section.

Part 3 · Their people

The family around them

A child’s story is also shaped by the people who show up, the names they are called, and the love repeated in ordinary ways.

Part 4 · Ordinary magic

The moments time might otherwise take

Think beyond milestones. Often it is the untied shoelace, the bedtime request, the car song, or the way they wait at the window that becomes priceless later.

Part 5 · Heart and growth

What should the story quietly give them?

We do not lecture children or turn struggles into labels. We use warmth, example, and age-appropriate moments to strengthen the beliefs and skills you want them to carry.

Part 6 · Voice and belonging

Culture, faith, and the language of home

The story should feel rooted in your family—not like a generic story with a name added.

Part 7 · The book they will hold

Personal touches and visual truth

Monarch Tales controls the premium visual style. Your details help us make the people, places, and meaningful objects feel true.

We will request clear reference photographs through the approved private process. Please do not send sensitive documents.

Part 8 · Care and boundaries

What should we handle gently—or leave out?

You are in control of what belongs in your family’s book. Clear boundaries help us create safely and respectfully.

Part 9 · A letter across time

One last pause

Imagine the child opening this book twenty years from now. They look up and ask, “What were you hoping I would always remember?”

Thank you for slowing down and remembering. From here, we turn these details into a story that could belong only to your family.

You’ll review your story before it’s finalized.

Why this form matters

A photograph remembers a face. These answers help us remember the voice, habits, relationships, values, and feelings around it.

What this step captures

  • How each child speaks, moves, loves, and feels safe
  • The family routines and tiny behaviours time can erase
  • The moral, emotional, cultural, and faith foundations
  • The people, places, and objects the illustrations should preserve

Your place is reserved

This private story discovery begins after acceptance. Your answers guide planning; they do not replace your story review and character approval stages.

How to answer well

Use exact words when you remember them. Describe what happened rather than trying to explain what it means. Skip optional questions that do not fit your family.
