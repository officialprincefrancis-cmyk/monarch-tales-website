For organisations serving families

Help a family preserve a story worth keeping.

Monarch Tales works with selected partners who already serve families, children, travellers or people looking for meaningful gifts.

A thoughtful fit

Make a meaningful memory easier to keep.

We are open to conversations with family photographers, bookstores, gift retailers, hotels, tourism businesses, community organisations and relevant family brands.

Possible starting points include referral collaborations, gift experiences, retail voucher pilots, family-memory collaborations, destination-memory research, and print or fulfilment partnerships.

Each collaboration is scoped individually. No commission, exclusivity or paid-media arrangement is assumed unless both parties agree in writing.

Good partnership conversations start with

  • The families or customers you serve
  • The moment or need your audience has
  • What a useful, low-risk pilot might look like
  • Any timing, region or fulfilment considerations

A separate pathway

Gifted story collaborations

A gifted collaboration is an individually agreed project with a creator, family or partner. It is separate from the paid Founding Families places and does not use the Founding Families deposit or benefits.

Before any story form is shared, Monarch Tales and the collaborator agree the deliverables, dates, book or digital format, any content expectations, privacy boundaries and the adult or guardian’s role. A parent or legal guardian must provide permission before child details or photographs are collected.

The family memory questionnaire is shared only when the project is ready for story creation and the appropriate parent or guardian has agreed. Gifted does not mean a public endorsement is required; any content or usage rights must be agreed separately in writing.
